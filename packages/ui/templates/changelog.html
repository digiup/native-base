What’s new in Orbit

New features, improvements and fixes to the product analytics platform, shipped every week.

RSS
  1. v4.2 · October 7, 2026

    Funnels you can branch

    NewImproved

    A funnel step can now split into several paths, so you can compare how people who invite a teammate convert against those who import data first. Conversion is shown per branch and for the funnel as a whole.

    • Pricing page 100%
    • Started trial 38%
    • Invited team 21%
    • Upgraded 9%
    • Drag a step onto another to create a branch.
    • Funnels load up to four times faster on projects with over 1 billion events.
    • Export any funnel as CSV, including per-branch breakdowns.

    Fixed

    • Scheduled CSV exports respect the project’s time zone.
    • The event picker keeps its scroll position after a search.

  4. v4.0 · September 2, 2026

    Orbit 4: a new query engine

    NewImproved

    We rebuilt the engine behind every chart on columnar storage. Most queries now return in under a second, even across years of data, and sampling is off by default. Benchmarks below ran on a project with 2.1 billion events.

    Weekly actives0.4 s92%

    Was 4.8 s

    Five-step funnel0.9 s92%

    Was 11.2 s

    12-week retention1.1 s85%

    Was 7.5 s

    • Breakdowns support up to 10,000 values instead of 300.
    • Saved insights migrate automatically; nothing to change on your side.
    • The old engine stays available under Settings until December 1.
  5. v3.9 · August 12, 2026

    Alerts in Slack and Microsoft Teams

    New

    Set a threshold on any metric and Orbit posts to a channel when it is crossed, with a link to the chart and the deploy that preceded the change.
