Questions about billing

Anything else? Email billing@cadence.app and a human replies within a working day.

Who counts as a seat on the Team plan?

Anyone with their own booking page or who can be assigned meetings. Admins who only manage settings and view reports are free.

Can I switch between monthly and yearly billing?

Yes, from your billing settings. Moving to yearly takes effect immediately and we credit the unused part of your current month.

What happens when a trial ends?

You move to the Personal plan unless you add a card. Your event types stay, and paid-only features pause until you upgrade.

Do you offer discounts for nonprofits and education?

Registered nonprofits and accredited schools get 50% off Pro and Team. Apply with your organisation's email address.
