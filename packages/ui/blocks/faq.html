FAQ

Questions before you switch

Moving deploys is a big decision. Here is what teams ask us most. Anything else, talk to an engineer and get an answer the same day.

How long does a migration from our current CI take?

Most teams deploy their first app within an hour. Relay detects the framework and build command; you add environment variables and point your domain. Large monorepos usually take a week, with our engineers pairing on it.

Can we keep our existing cloud account?

Yes. On Team and Enterprise, Relay can deploy into your own AWS or GCP account, so data and spend stay where they are today.

What happens to previews when a branch merges?

The preview URL and its database branch are deleted ten minutes after merge. Pin a preview to keep it for a demo.

Do you charge for build minutes?

No. Plans are priced per seat. Builds, previews and rollbacks are unlimited within fair use.

Is there an on-premises option?

Enterprise customers can run the Relay control plane in a private VPC. Contact sales for the architecture review.

Frequently asked questions

Everything about billing, trials and your data.

Do I need a credit card to start the trial?

No. The 14-day Team trial starts without a card. You choose a plan at the end, or drop back to Hobby automatically.

Can I change plans later?

Anytime. Upgrades take effect immediately and are prorated; downgrades apply at the next billing date.

Where is my data stored?

In the region you pick when you create a workspace: Frankfurt, Virginia or Sydney. It does not leave that region.

Do you offer discounts for startups and nonprofits?

Startups under two years old and registered nonprofits get 50% off Team for the first year. Apply from the billing page.

How do I cancel?

From Settings, Billing, Cancel plan. Your workspace stays readable for 30 days so you can export everything.
